Tic-Tac-Toe

Take turns marking a square on a three-by-three grid. The first to get three in a row, across, down or diagonally, wins.

How to play

Games are played with a contact in the FlickerTalk app: open the Games tab, or tap the 🎮 button in a chat, and the match runs between the two phones of that conversation. You need FlickerTalk 1.3.0 or newer. Games are available on Android for now, from Google Play.

Privacy

Matches travel end-to-end encrypted between the two phones, over the same connection as your chat, and are saved only on them. If that connection has to go through our relay, the relay carries the encrypted data and cannot read it.
