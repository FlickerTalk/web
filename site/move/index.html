Move FlickerTalk to a new phone

This link comes from the code FlickerTalk shows on a new phone. When your old phone reads it, it moves your identity, contacts and messages to the new one.

How to move

  1. On the new phone, get FlickerTalk. Open it and tap “I have FlickerTalk on another phone” to show the code. If it already shows the code, skip this step.
  2. On the old phone, open FlickerTalk. Go to Settings → “Move to a new phone”.
  3. Read the new phone’s code. Tap “Scan the new phone's code” and scan it. Or copy the address of this page from the address bar (it is the whole link), paste it into “Or paste its link” and tap “Move and erase”. Once everything has moved, the old phone is erased.

Your browser does not send the part of the link after # to our server.
