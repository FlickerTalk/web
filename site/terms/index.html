2. The service

FlickerTalk lets you exchange text, files and voice and video calls with another person, from phone to phone, and text messages with a small group of your contacts, called a circle. What you write in a circle reaches every member, and its admins decide who is in it. Our servers only wake phones, help them connect, keep undelivered messages, encrypted end to end, for up to 7 days, and forward the suggestions you send from the app to us. See How it works.

If you lose your phone, uninstall the app or erase it, we cannot recover your messages, your contacts or your identity: we keep no copy of them. The app can keep a backup in your own Google Drive, encrypted on your phone and opened on another phone only with the recovery phrase you choose; restoring it brings back your phone as it was when the backup was made. Without that phrase nobody can open it on another phone, and we cannot recover the phrase for you. To change phones, use “Move to a new phone” while you still have the old one (see the FAQ).

5. Zero tolerance for abuse

FlickerTalk has no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Do not use FlickerTalk to break the law or to harm others: no harassment, threats, spam, malware, sexual content involving minors or any other illegal content, and no attempts to overload, attack or misuse our servers.

We cannot read what people send each other: messages are encrypted end to end, and we store no conversation history on our servers. So these are the ways to stop abuse:

  • Block: on the contact's page. It works at once and only on your phone, without going through our servers: your phone closes any direct connection with that contact and from then on discards their messages and calls. In a circle, its admins can also remove any member.
  • Report: on the contact's page, tap Report, choose a reason and, if you want, add their last messages as evidence. It opens an email from your own mail app to info@flickertalk.com with their FlickerTalk ID, the reason and only the evidence you chose to include, and it blocks them on your phone. You can also write to that address yourself.

We review every report and act on it within 24 hours. When a report shows abuse, we cut the reported device off from our servers: it can no longer register, be woken up or use the mailbox. We also tell whoever reported it to block that contact, if they have not already. Messages that were already delivered are only on the phones that received them, where you can delete them.
