Private messages, phone to phone

Text, files, voice and video calls between two people. Your conversations live on your phones, not on our servers.

🔑

No account

No phone number, no email, no password. Your identity is a key made on your phone. You add people by scanning their code or opening their link.

📱

Phone to phone

Messages go straight to the other phone whenever it can be reached, encrypted end to end.

📬

Delivered, not stored

If the other phone is off, the message waits in a mailbox, encrypted so that we cannot read it, until it is picked up or 7 days pass. You can turn the mailbox off.

📞

Files and calls

Voice and video calls and files of any size, always one to one, and never through the mailbox.

Price

The first year is free, counted from the day you install it, and no card is asked for.
