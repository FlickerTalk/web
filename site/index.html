No sign-up. Just a connection.

No email, phone number or password. Add someone by scanning their code or opening their link. Your address book stays yours.

🚫

No activity logs. By design.

We do not keep a history of your connections, calls or IP addresses. We do not track your activity or build a profile of you.

📱

Your history stays with you.

Your conversations are stored on your phones. We do not keep a server-side copy of your chat history or a cloud backup.

📬

Private, even while waiting.

If the other phone is offline, an optional mailbox holds your message end-to-end encrypted. It is deleted when picked up, or after 7 days at the latest. We cannot read it.

Everyday conversations. Less to give away.

Send a message, share a file or hear a familiar voice. FlickerTalk is built for conversations between two people.

  • Text messages.
  • File sharing.
  • Voice and video calls.
  • One-to-one conversations.

Messages travel directly between phones when possible. Encrypted relays help when a direct connection is unavailable. Files and calls are never stored in the offline mailbox.

Small tools. Kept separate.

Make a picture smaller. Cover a face or a number. Turn pictures into a PDF. Sketch an idea or write in Markdown.

None of them comes inside the app: install only the ones you want, from Settings. Each tool runs in isolation, without network access, and only sees the picture or text you hand it, never your chat history, your contacts or your keys.

Privacy includes what we choose not to keep.

We keep only the technical information needed to recognise your device, help your contacts reach you and deliver pending messages. We do not ask for your real-world identity or keep activity logs.
