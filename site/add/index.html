You are invited to FlickerTalk

Someone invited you to talk on FlickerTalk, a private messenger with no account and no phone number. To add them, paste their link in the app.

How to add them

  1. Get FlickerTalk. If you already have FlickerTalk, skip this step.

    FlickerTalk is not on the App Store yet. The iPhone version is coming soon.

  2. Copy the link. Tap your browser’s address bar and copy the address of this page: it is the whole link, even if the bar shows only part of it. Copying just the link from the message you received works too.
  3. Paste it in the app. Open FlickerTalk, tap “Add contact” (the QR code button at the top of Chats) and choose “Scan”. Then paste it into “Or paste their link” and tap “Add”.

Your browser does not send the part of the link after # to our server.
